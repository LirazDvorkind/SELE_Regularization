AI Summary:
The parametric model is the best method on this test set, especially in the first 3 µm, which is the part of the profile the ELE measurement actually constrains.

The test set is 17 ground-truth SELE curves: 6 surface-recombination-velocity curves and 11 lifetime curves. Every method gets the same measured ELE and is scored
over 0–30 µm.

Method by method:
- Parametric model: about 1% error in the first µm, about 9% at 1–3 µm, and a surface value within about 2%. It gets the peak depth within about 1 µm and the peak
    height within about 16%. Beyond about 8 µm the errors grow to around 30%, but no method is really measured there.
- Tikhonov: its automatic κ choice (the L-curve corner) fails on every curve, and the profiles come out orders of magnitude off. With κ picked using the truth it
    does about as well as the parametric model overall, but it is worse near the surface: 21% error in the first µm and 53% on the surface value. That truth-picked
    result is only a best case, not something a real measurement allows.
- Score model: worst on every metric, at roughly 40–80% error in every depth zone. It returns a nearly flat profile near the measured ELE level and misses the shape.
    It still reproduces the ELE well, which shows how many different profiles fit the same measurement.
